# Form a question and an evidence plan

## The engineering or business decision

Research automation needs a question that can be answered from identifiable evidence. Define the decision you are supporting, inclusion rules, publication-date limits, and what would count as contradictory information. A list of links is not a synthesis, and a model’s familiarity with a topic is not source verification.

Choose a low-stakes product or technical comparison with public sources. Prefer primary documentation for factual claims. Record when you accessed a page and whether the information describes an announced feature, an available feature, or an observed behavior. Avoid copying entire copyrighted articles into your project; preserve short relevant evidence notes and source links.

## Worked example

Instead of “research AI tools,” ask “Which of these three local document parsers supports our required text formats without a hosted service?” Define required formats, offline behavior, license, and failure reporting before collecting evidence. Unknown support remains unknown until documented or tested.

## Build it yourself

1. Write one decision question and a comparison matrix with five criteria.
2. Choose three candidate tools and primary source pages.
3. Capture claim, source URL, access date, supporting passage summary, and uncertainty.
4. Create one contradictory-source example and explain how you will resolve or present it.

## Break it deliberately

A vendor landing page promises support while its technical docs list a limitation. Preserve the disagreement instead of averaging it into a confident yes.

## Acceptance checks

- Each comparison criterion supports a real decision.
- Claims have source identity and access date.
- Unknown and contradictory evidence remain visible.

## Deliverable

Save **`studio-03/evidence-plan.md`** in your separate program workspace. Include your inputs, actual outputs, decisions, and unresolved limitations. Budget about **150 minutes** for reading, implementation and review; this is an estimate, not a timed requirement.
