# Design a taxonomy with an uncertainty route

## The engineering or business decision

Classification automates a decision boundary. Define categories by the downstream action they enable, not merely by similar wording. A category is useful when its definition and examples help two people agree on the right handling. Include an uncertainty or review route when input does not establish a safe choice.

This project routes fictional support messages without sending replies or changing customer records. Urgency and topic are separate fields. A complaint about a delayed shipment may be a shipping topic and a normal priority; an account compromise may require an urgent human response regardless of tone. Avoid using sentiment as a substitute for operational severity.

## Worked example

A message says “Please update the address on my order, and I cannot access my account.” A single-label topic classifier hides one task. Decide whether to split into two reviewable intents or route to a multi-issue queue. Document that policy before scoring output.

## Build it yourself

1. Create a taxonomy of five support topics plus review and multi-issue behavior.
2. Write 30 synthetic messages, including short, ambiguous, and multi-intent cases.
3. Label topic, urgency, and allowed next action independently.
4. Review disagreements and revise category definitions before implementation.

## Break it deliberately

A calmly worded message reports a stolen account. The workflow must not assign low priority merely because the wording is polite.

## Acceptance checks

- Topic and urgency are separate labels.
- Every category has inclusion and exclusion examples.
- Multi-intent and ambiguous messages have a defined path.
