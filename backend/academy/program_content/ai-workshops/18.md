# Deliver a portfolio of practical workflows

## The engineering or business decision

A portfolio should explain what each workflow does, how you tested it, and where it fails. Six superficial demos are less useful than documented prototypes with clear trade-offs. For each studio, include one representative input, output, evaluation summary, and next improvement. Keep all example data synthetic or explicitly permissioned.

Your final app can be the strongest workflow, supported by the other five as focused explorations. Avoid promising production reliability from workshop-scale evidence. A recipient should be able to reproduce the main example and understand which steps are manual, simulated, or integrated with a real provider.

## Worked example

A portfolio case study shows the support triage baseline, its confusion matrix, a multi-intent failure, the revised review queue, and the result of replaying a duplicate event. This demonstrates judgment and implementation more clearly than a video of a single predicted label.

## Build it yourself

1. Create a portfolio index linking all six studio artifacts.
2. Write one detailed case study for the final app with observed evidence.
3. Have another person run the setup and complete a task without coaching.
4. Create a maintenance backlog ordered by user impact and remaining failure risk.

## Break it deliberately

The reviewer cannot reproduce an output because a fixture was omitted. Add the missing fixture and rerun the documented command from a clean workspace.

## Acceptance checks

- All six studios have specific artifacts and limitations.
- One complete application is reproducible.
- The case study includes a failed example and a justified correction.

## Deliverable

Save **`portfolio/README.md`** in your separate program workspace. Include your inputs, actual outputs, decisions, and unresolved limitations. Budget about **180 minutes** for reading, implementation and review; this is an estimate, not a timed requirement.

## Explain your judgment

Which acceptance check would change your decision if it failed? Explain what your artifact demonstrates and what it cannot establish. Completion in this academy records your own report; the platform does not execute your work or certify it.
