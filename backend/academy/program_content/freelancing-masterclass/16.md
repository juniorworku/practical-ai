# Write an evidence-based case study

## The engineering or business decision

A credible case study explains the client context, constraints, decisions, implementation, observed results, and remaining limitations. Ask for permission before using real client names, logos, quotes, or data. A self-directed project is valid when labeled accurately. Do not invent testimonials or imply a simulated buyer is a paying customer.

Use the same baseline and evaluation definitions from discovery. If the pilot improved one measure but worsened another, include the trade-off. Screenshots can illustrate a workflow, but they do not replace evidence of persistence, access control, or factual support. Show the artifact that substantiates each technical claim.

## Worked example

“Simulated engagement for a training provider: draft review time fell from eight to six minutes on ten synthetic notes, while two outputs required source clarification.” This is a scoped observation. It does not establish market demand or predict a client’s future savings.

## Build it yourself

1. Write a case study using only synthetic or permissioned artifacts.
2. Map each result claim to a baseline, evaluation record, or test.
3. Include one failed attempt and the decision it changed.
4. Review all labels for simulated versus actual work.

## Break it deliberately

A portfolio draft says “helped a client” when the engagement was fictional. Correct it and preserve the value through specific implementation evidence.

## Acceptance checks

- No invented customer, result, or testimonial appears.
- Claims include sample size and scope.
- A failure and its correction are explained.

## Deliverable

Save **`client-06/case-study.md`** in your separate program workspace. Include your inputs, actual outputs, decisions, and unresolved limitations. Budget about **120 minutes** for reading, implementation and review; this is an estimate, not a timed requirement.

## Explain your judgment

Which acceptance check would change your decision if it failed? Explain what your artifact demonstrates and what it cannot establish. Completion in this academy records your own report; the platform does not execute your work or certify it.
